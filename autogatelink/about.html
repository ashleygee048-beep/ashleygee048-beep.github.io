About Autogatelink

15+ years of precision engineering. A professional manufacturer you can trust.

Driven by Quality,
Powered by Innovation

Autogatelink Limited is a professional manufacturer specializing in gate automation systems. With over 15 years of industry experience, we design, produce, and export high-quality garage door openers and sliding gate motors to markets worldwide.

Our factory operates across modern production lines with a rigorous quality control system. Every unit undergoes 100% testing before shipment, ensuring reliability you can count on.

We serve distributors, contractors, and OEM partners in over 50 countries — from Europe and North America to Southeast Asia and the Middle East.
