A5 vs K8: Which Garage Door Opener Fits Your Door?

Two opener platforms, four force ratings, two drive options. Here is how to pick between the A5 STD series and the K8 Plus Pro series using nothing but the door on your project.

Specifying a sectional garage door opener usually comes down to four numbers: the force you need, the area and weight of the door, the height it has to lift, and the speed the client expects. Everything else — design, lighting, remote frequency — is secondary.

Autogatelink builds two opener platforms. They share the same electronics philosophy but target different ends of the market. This guide walks through the real specifications of both so you can pick without guessing.

The shared foundation

Both the A5 STD series and the K8 Plus Pro series are built on the same platform assumptions:

Input voltage220-240V / 110-127V, 50-60Hz220-240V / 110-127V, 50-60Hz
Max. door height2400-5000 mm2400-5000 mm
Remote frequency433MHz / 868MHz433MHz / 868MHz

Both ranges are CE and RCM certified for the European and Australian markets, meet EN 12453 and EN 12445, and include the options needed to make the opener compatible with home automation systems.

So on voltage, radio and compliance there is nothing to choose between them. The differences are force, capacity, drive rail and design.

A5 STD series — the value workhorse

The A5 uses a practical industrial design. The detail that matters more than it sounds: the LED bulbs are easier to replace. On a value-priced opener, lamp replacement is one of the most common service calls, so a design that makes it a two-minute job has a real cost-of-ownership effect over a ten-year life.

The A5 comes in two force ratings, each available in a standard and a speed variant:

The speed variant is the interesting option. Going from 160 mm/s to 200 mm/s is a 25% reduction in travel time, which residents notice on a 2.4-3 m door every single day. If the client complains about waiting for the door, the speed variant solves it without buying a bigger motor.

Pick the A5 when: the door is a standard residential sectional up to 13 ㎡ / 150 kg, the budget is the deciding factor, and the client cares about serviceability rather than styling.

K8 Plus Pro series — the design-led platform

The K8 has an innovative matte-finish housing accented by two parallel, horizontal LED light strips. It is not just an opener — it reads as a decorative piece that lifts the whole garage interior. That is the reason to specify it, and it is worth being honest about: you buy the K8 for how it looks and what it says about the house, and you get a very capable opener at the same time.

Two structural differences are worth flagging:

One-piece iron bottom casing. Instead of plastic, the base of the K8 head unit is a single iron pressing. That is a meaningful difference on wide doors, where the head unit carries more of the load and vibration over time.

T rail availability. The K8 can be supplied on T rail as well as C rail. T rail is the more rigid option and is the usual choice once you move into the 1200N and 1500N sizes and the larger door areas that come with them.

Pick the K8 when: the door runs up to 20 ㎡ / 210 kg, the installation is visible and the client is buying on design, or the project needs the extra rigidity of a T rail.

A simple decision rule

  1. Door up to 13 ㎡ / 150 kg, price-sensitive, standard residential → A5.
  2. Same door, but the client wants it to move faster → A5 speed variant.
  3. Door between 13 and 20 ㎡, or 180-210 kg → K8, 1200N or 1500N.
  4. Visible installation, high-end residential, show home → K8 regardless of door size, for the design and the iron casing.
  5. Needs T rail → K8.

What to send us for a quote

To quote accurately we need: door type (sectional, one-piece, tilt), door width and height, door weight if known, available headroom, single or double door, supply voltage at site, and the quantity per model. With those six data points we can confirm the force rating and rail without a second round of questions.

Both platforms are available for OEM and ODM projects, with the accessory range — replacement gears, universal remotes, receivers and Wi-Fi control — supplied alongside any opener order.
