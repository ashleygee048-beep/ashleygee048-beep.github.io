STD Series

A5 STD Garage Door Opener

The STD series garage door opener uses a practical industrial design, and its LED bulbs are easier to replace. It delivers a compressive and tensile force of 800N and 1000N, and both are available in a speed variant — providing faster opening and closing of the garage door.

  • Max. force800N / 1000N
  • Max. door weight100 / 150 kg
  • Max. door area10 / 13 ㎡
  • Opening speed160 / 200 mm/s
  • Input voltage220-240V / 110-127V
  • Remote frequency433 / 868 MHz
  • Max. door height2400-5000 mm
  • Applicable trackC rail, chain or belt drive

Plus Pro Series

K8 Plus Pro Garage Door Opener
